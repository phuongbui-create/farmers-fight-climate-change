# Farmers Fight (Climate Change): Too Hot to Work?

**Live site:** https://phuongbui-create.github.io/farmers-fight-climate-change/

An interactive heat planner for farm work. Set the forecast air temperature, humidity and wind speed, choose the worker's age group and sun or shade, and the page shows the hardest work (in METs) someone can keep up all day without their body storing heat.

The page includes:

- **A verdict panel** showing the highest safe sustained activity, the wet-bulb temperature, and whether humid air or sweat rate is the limit.
- **A livability map** of max sustained activity across temperature and humidity, with the 31 °C and 35 °C wet-bulb lines marked.
- **A wind chart** showing when wind helps and when it starts adding heat.
- **A farm-task table** showing which jobs (driving a tractor, moving pipe, hoeing, stacking hay) can be sustained right now.

The model is a simplified version of the human heat-balance model in Vanos et al. (2023), *Nature Communications* 14, 7653, with wet-bulb temperature from Stull (2011). It's a teaching tool, not a medical device.

## Running locally

It's a single self-contained HTML file with no dependencies. Open `index.html` in any browser.
